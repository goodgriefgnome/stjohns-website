## Are you new?

Getting involved in a church can be intimidating. You may have never been to church before and don’t know what to expect. Sadly, some people have had damaging experiences at a church in the past and so want to be especially cautious. Whatever your experience, we want to make it easy and safe for you to learn about St John’s.

Take your time to read our website and please reach out using the form below. We're looking forward to meeting you.
