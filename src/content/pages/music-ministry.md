## Music Ministry

:::column
Musical worship is one of the most important things that happens in the church. The songs we sing together are not mere window dressing, but rather have a decisive role to play in shaping our beliefs and forming our souls. It is often the music that takes the teaching of the gospel beyond being merely information in our heads, and plants it as life-changing truth deep in our hearts. Therefore, we place a high value on musical worship when we gather each week.

![Musicians leading worship](/media/b82d99f45a3b22dd843d.webp)
:::

:::column
The style of music at St John’s is not monolithic, but rather eclectic, drawing on a wealth of traditions ranging from contemporary songs accompanied by guitar or piano, to ancient hymns accompanied by our 1885 Hill & Son organ. On high holy days the choir may also be heard leading congregational hymns and singing special pieces for reflection and worship.

Our music minister and musicians work to choose hymns and songs to best serve our diverse community at each stage of its life, helping all of us to enter God’s presence with praise, confession, thanksgiving, and lament.

## Choir

St John’s choir is a vital part of our music program, offering an opportunity for individuals of all ages and skill levels to engage with the rich Anglican choral tradition. The choir rehearses on Sundays after the morning service and sings regularly for services including Evensong (as part of our Twilight Music Series), Ash Wednesday, Easter, Advent, Christmas, and other Sunday services at St John's.

If you are interested in joining the choir, [click here](https://form.jotform.com/231380582288057).
