## We're here to help with our new Community Pantry

Please come and join us for tea, coffee and biscuits every other Wednesday between 10am and 11am.\
Our Community Pantry offers two bags of groceries worth $50 for $15 each.

The Community Pantry will start on Wednesday 11 February and then every other Wednesday through the year.
